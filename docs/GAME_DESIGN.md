# HUSH BELOW!

## Core promise

The player pilots a tiny research sub through caves that are almost completely dark. Pressing A sends an expanding sonar pulse that reveals rock, specimens, and hazards for a moment. The same pulse gives hunting creatures an exact target. Information is safety and danger at once.

## Controls

- D-pad: thrust in four directions
- A: sonar ping
- B: kill engines and brake
- Start: pause
- Select: dive log

## Campaign

Three dives form a complete arcade story:

1. Shelf: recover three luminous specimens and return to the buoy.
2. Wreck: recover three signal fragments through tighter rock and more hunters.
3. Abyss: activate the lost transmitter and surface before oxygen expires.

Free Dive follows the same rules without a terminal campaign clock. A dive report records specimens, pings, collisions, and remaining oxygen. The ending changes with recovery performance.

## Boundaries

This is not a routing game, a city simulation, an upgrade economy, or a combat game. There are no weapons. Mastery comes from reading the fading pulse, coasting silently, and choosing when knowledge is worth exposure.
